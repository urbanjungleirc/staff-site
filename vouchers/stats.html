How fast a cohort comes back

Of the value sold in each month, how much had been redeemed by 1, 3, 6 and 12 months. The bands are cumulative, so they are drawn side by side, never stacked.

A band is only drawn once the cohort is old enough to have one. A voucher sold last month cannot yet have a 12-month figure, so no 12-month bar is drawn for it — that gap is the honest answer, not a zero. Cohort months marked * are still younger than 12 months and therefore incomplete.

How to read the earlier years. Redemptions before June 2026 were reconstructed from voucher balances at migration.
