1.Personalised Invitation

Use our online form to personalise your Urban Jungle Birthday invitation and share with guests.
2.Send List of Participants

Email us the first & last names of all climbers at least 3 days before your booking. Remember to keep within the maximum capacity so all kids have a safe experience.
3.Complete Waivers

Ensure all participants complete the online waiver before the event. Those under 18 require a parent/guardian signature.
4.Organise Belayers

Make sure you have enough belayers (age 12+) in your group. The more belayers, the more climbers can be on the wall.
5.Decorate & Celebrate

Bring decorations and food of your choice to make it your own. We’ll have the coffee machine ready for adults!
